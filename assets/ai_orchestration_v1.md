# AI Orchestration V1

## Purpose

AI Orchestration defines how artificial intelligence operates within the Cryptic Vision platform.

AI is treated as a governed engineering runtime rather than an autonomous system.

Its purpose is to assist observation, analysis, planning, and engineering workflows while remaining under explicit human control.

The objective is to provide repeatable, verifiable, and expandable AI-assisted engineering.

# AI Responsibilities

AI may:

• analyze engineering data

• summarize information

• generate reports

• recommend actions

• assist planning

• explain system behavior

• classify engineering observations

• translate engineering knowledge

AI performs reasoning.

AI does not govern the platform.

---

# AI Boundaries

AI does not:

• silently execute commands

• modify verified memory

• overwrite reports

• bypass permissions

• change runtime ownership

• ignore verification

• perform unauthorized actions

Every AI action remains observable.

---

# AI Inputs

AI receives verified information from:

• Memory Runtime

• Event Runtime

• Reports

• State files

• Bridge files

• Runtime outputs

AI operates on engineering evidence.

Unknown information remains under observation until verified.

---

# AI Outputs

AI may produce:

• engineering summaries

• recommendations

• planning documents

• runtime explanations

• patient observations

• exported reports

• prompt packets

• structured data

Outputs remain subject to human verification.

---

# AI Model Independence

Cryptic Vision is model independent.

Supported models may include:

• local models

• cloud models

• future providers

• specialized engineering models

The orchestration layer remains stable regardless of the underlying AI provider.

Replacing a model should not require redesigning the platform.
